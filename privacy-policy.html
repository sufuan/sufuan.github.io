Privacy Policy — Sufian's Lab (SkaleUp)

This app is operated for SkaleUp, a dashboard that helps online sellers manage customer messages and orders from Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp in one place.

What we collect: when a customer messages a connected Facebook Page or WhatsApp number, we receive the message content, the sender's name and phone number or profile id, and order details the customer shares.

Why: to show the conversation to the seller, to draft or send replies the seller approves, and to create orders the customer asks for.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use message data for advertising. Data is stored on the SkaleUp server while the seller's account is active.

A customer or seller can ask for their data to be deleted at any time — see the Data Deletion page.
